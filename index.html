LiveLock™

Inclusive Smart Door System for Accessible Living

The Problem

Many individuals experience physical limitations that make traditional doors difficult to use. Challenges include gripping door handles, turning keys, navigating tight spaces, and opening doors while using mobility aids. These barriers affect elderly users and people living with arthritis, carpal tunnel, limb loss, or temporary physical limitations.

The Solution

LiveLock™ is an intelligent interior door designed with empathy and inclusion in mind. It integrates biometric access, smart panels, and voice activation to create a door that responds to the user without requiring physical force.

Key Features

Voice Activated Open & Close
Fingerprint Recognition
Facial Recognition
Palm Pattern Scanning
Smartphone App Control
Emergency Auto-Unlock (Fire Safety)
Bluetooth Coffee Trigger
Manual Lever Handle Backup

Built-In Safety & Reliability

  • Passive infrared sensors for emergency response
  • Key-aid inspired manual locking system
